## Assessment Parameters
- **Target Audience:** IBM Consulting FutureNow Center Philippines | Quality Management System Team
- **Source Material:** 2026 Issue Management Policy and Process Training_v1_FNC (slides 1–18)
- **Pool Size:** 15 questions grounded in the training content
- **Draw per Attempt:** 10 questions randomly selected
- **Passing Threshold:** 85% (9 out of 10)
- **Question Types:** Multiple Choice, True/False, Fill-in-the-Blank, Workplace Scenarios
- **Security Logic:** Correct answers are never revealed on failure
- **Retakes:** Unlimited retakes with fresh randomization
